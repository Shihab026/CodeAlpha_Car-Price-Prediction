# CodeAlpha_Car-Price-Prediction

onclusion:

The trained RandomForestRegressor model has demonstrated excellent performance in predicting car selling prices. With an R-squared (R2) score of 0.96, the model can explain 96% of the variance in the selling prices, indicating a very strong fit to the data. The Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 0.61 suggests that, on average, the model's predictions are off by approximately 0.61 units (e.g., Lakhs if the Selling_Price is in Lakhs). This low MAE, combined with the high R2 score, implies that the model is highly accurate and can be reliably used for predicting car prices based on the features available in this dataset. This level of accuracy makes the model suitable for real-world applications in car dealerships, online marketplaces, or for individuals looking to buy or sell used cars, enabling them to estimate fair prices.
